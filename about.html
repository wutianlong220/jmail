About JMail.best

Our Mission

JMail.best is dedicated to providing public access to the Jeffrey Epstein email archives, ensuring transparency and preserving historical documentation for researchers, journalists, and the public.

Why We Created This Archive

The Jeffrey Epstein case represents one of the most significant legal and social stories of the 21st century. The emails from this case offer unprecedented insights into networks of power, influence, and operations that have shaped public understanding of these events.

Our mission is built on three core principles:

  • Transparency: Making public records easily accessible to everyone
  • Preservation: Ensuring historical documents are permanently archived
  • Education: Facilitating research and informed public discourse

Email Sources and Authenticity

All emails in this archive are obtained from official sources, including:

  • Court documents and legal filings
  • Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests
  • Public record releases
  • Official government disclosures

We are committed to maintaining the integrity and authenticity of these documents. Every email in our collection is sourced from verifiable, official channels.
