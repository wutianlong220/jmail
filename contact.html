Before You Reach Out

Check Our FAQ First

Many common questions are answered on our FAQ page, including:

  • How to search and browse the archive
  • Information about email sources and authenticity
  • Technical questions about using the site
  • Legal and privacy-related questions

Response Times

We strive to respond to all inquiries within 2-3 business days. Response times may vary depending on the nature of your inquiry and volume of requests.

What to Include in Your Message

To help us assist you more effectively, please include:

  • Your name and affiliation (if applicable)
  • A clear subject line describing your inquiry
  • Detailed information about your question or request
  • Any relevant context or background information

Media Inquiries

We welcome media inquiries and are happy to provide:

  • Background information on the archive
  • Comments for news stories and reports
  • Expert insights on the email collection
  • Interview opportunities (subject to availability)

Please use media@jmail.best for all press-related inquiries and include your media affiliation, deadline, and specific questions.

Research and Academic Inquiries

We support academic and journalistic research and are available to assist with:

  • Navigating the email archive
  • Understanding the collection's scope and limitations
  • Providing context on specific documents or time periods
  • Discussing research methodologies

For research inquiries, please contact us at research@jmail.best with details about your project or research goals.

Report an Issue

If you've discovered a technical issue, broken link, or error on the website, please let us know. Include:

  • The page or feature where you encountered the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the problem
  • Your browser and device information
  • Screenshots (if applicable)

Email technical issues to support@jmail.best.
